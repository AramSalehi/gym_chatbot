# Frequently asked questions
**Q: Is there free Wi-Fi?** Yes. Ask reception for the current guest password.
**Q: Is parking available?** Yes, 20 free spaces for members, first-come first-served. Bicycle racks are beside the entrance.
**Q: Can I bring children?** No. Entry and membership are restricted to adults aged 18+; there is no childcare.
**Q: Do you have a pool or sauna?** No.
**Q: Can beginners attend classes?** Yes. Strength Foundations is specifically for beginners; all-level classes are also suitable with modifications.
**Q: Can I join without a membership?** Yes, purchase a $15 day pass or a $25 seven-day trial.
**Q: Can I book a class by phone?** Yes, reception can help with class bookings during opening hours.
**Q: Do I need to bring a towel?** Bring your own or rent one for $2 per visit.
