# Membership policies
## Cancellation
Monthly memberships can be canceled with 14 calendar days' notice before the next billing date. Submit requests by email to hello@ironharbor.example or in person; reception confirms the effective date in writing. No partial-month refunds after a billing cycle starts.
## Freeze
Members can freeze a membership for 1 to 3 consecutive months once per rolling 12 months. Freeze requests require 7 days' notice before the next billing date; no fee during the approved freeze. Access and class booking are paused.
## Guests
Premium includes 2 guest passes each billing month. Other members can buy a $15 day pass for a guest. Guests must be at least 18 and sign a waiver.
## Lost property
Items are held for 30 days, then donated where appropriate. Valuables should not be left unattended.
