# Personal training
A complimentary 20-minute orientation is available to all new members; this is not a training session.
One-to-one personal training: $55 per 45-minute session. Five-session pack: $250, valid for 90 days from purchase. Partner training (2 people): $75 per 45-minute session total.
Trainer specialties: beginner lifting technique, general strength, conditioning, and mobility. Trainers do not provide medical diagnoses or individualized treatment.
Book through reception at least 24 hours ahead. Reschedule or cancel at least 12 hours before the appointment to avoid forfeiting the session. For health concerns or injuries, consult a qualified clinician before beginning training.
