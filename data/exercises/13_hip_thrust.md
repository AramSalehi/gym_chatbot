# Hip Thrust

Exercise ID: EX013
Category: Legs
Equipment: Bench and optional barbell
Primary muscles: Glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Upper back against stable bench, feet flat, weight padded if used.
2. Movement: Drive through feet and lift hips until torso and thighs align.
3. Return: Lower with control.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Hyperextending back; feet too far away.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Glute bridge. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
