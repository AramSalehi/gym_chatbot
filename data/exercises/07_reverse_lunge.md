# Reverse Lunge

Exercise ID: EX007
Category: Legs
Equipment: Bodyweight or dumbbells
Primary muscles: Quadriceps, glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand tall with feet hip-width.
2. Movement: Step one leg back, lower straight down with torso controlled.
3. Return: Push through front foot to return.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Too narrow stance; dropping hard onto back knee.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Split squat. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
