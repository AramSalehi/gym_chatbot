# Machine Chest Press

Exercise ID: EX020
Category: Chest
Equipment: Chest press machine
Primary muscles: Pectorals, triceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Adjust seat so handles align around mid-chest.
2. Movement: Press handles forward smoothly.
3. Return: Return until comfortable chest stretch.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Shrugging; seat too low.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Push-up. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
