# Bent-Over Barbell Row

Exercise ID: EX028
Category: Back
Equipment: Barbell
Primary muscles: Mid-back, lats

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Hinge at hips with braced neutral trunk.
2. Movement: Pull bar toward lower ribs.
3. Return: Lower bar under control.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Standing upright between reps; rounding back.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Chest-supported row. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
