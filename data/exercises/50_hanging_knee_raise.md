# Hanging Knee Raise

Exercise ID: EX050
Category: Core
Equipment: Pull-up bar or captain chair
Primary muscles: Abdominals, hip flexors

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Hang or support forearms, keep shoulders active.
2. Movement: Raise bent knees toward torso without swinging.
3. Return: Lower slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Swinging; excessive arch.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Reverse crunch. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
