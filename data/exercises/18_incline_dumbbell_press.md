# Incline Dumbbell Press

Exercise ID: EX018
Category: Chest
Equipment: Incline bench and dumbbells
Primary muscles: Upper pectorals, triceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Set bench at modest incline, feet planted.
2. Movement: Press dumbbells above upper chest.
3. Return: Lower slowly with controlled shoulders.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Excessive incline; shrugging.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Incline machine press. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
