# Hip Abduction Machine

Exercise ID: EX015
Category: Legs
Equipment: Hip abduction machine
Primary muscles: Gluteus medius

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Sit with back supported, knees against pads.
2. Movement: Move knees outward through comfortable range.
3. Return: Return slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Jerking weight; twisting trunk.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Banded lateral walk. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
