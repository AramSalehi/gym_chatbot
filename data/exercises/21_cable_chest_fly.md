# Cable Chest Fly

Exercise ID: EX021
Category: Chest
Equipment: Cable station
Primary muscles: Pectorals

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Set handles near chest height, stagger feet, soft elbows.
2. Movement: Bring hands together in front of chest in wide arc.
3. Return: Open arms slowly without overstretching.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Straightening elbows aggressively; torso swinging.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Pec deck. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
