# Chest-Supported Row

Exercise ID: EX029
Category: Back
Equipment: Incline bench and dumbbells
Primary muscles: Mid-back, lats

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Lie chest-down on incline bench with arms hanging.
2. Movement: Pull elbows back toward hips.
3. Return: Lower weights slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Shrugging; lifting chest off pad.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Seated cable row. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
