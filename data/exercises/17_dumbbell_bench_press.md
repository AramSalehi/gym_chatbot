# Dumbbell Bench Press

Exercise ID: EX017
Category: Chest
Equipment: Dumbbells and bench
Primary muscles: Pectorals, triceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Lie on bench, dumbbells near chest, wrists stacked.
2. Movement: Press weights upward and slightly inward.
3. Return: Lower with elbows in comfortable arc.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Dropping elbows too low; wrist bending.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Machine chest press. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
