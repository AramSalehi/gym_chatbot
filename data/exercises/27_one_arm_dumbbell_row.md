# One-Arm Dumbbell Row

Exercise ID: EX027
Category: Back
Equipment: Dumbbell and bench
Primary muscles: Lats, mid-back

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Support one hand and knee on bench, spine neutral.
2. Movement: Pull dumbbell toward hip with elbow close.
3. Return: Lower until arm extended.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Twisting torso; shrugging.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Cable single-arm row. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
