# Seated Leg Curl

Exercise ID: EX010
Category: Legs
Equipment: Seated leg curl machine
Primary muscles: Hamstrings

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Align knee with pivot, secure thigh and ankle pads.
2. Movement: Bend knees to pull lower legs down/back.
3. Return: Return slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Hips lifting; fast rebound.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Stability ball leg curl. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
