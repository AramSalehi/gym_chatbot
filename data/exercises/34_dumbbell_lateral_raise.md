# Dumbbell Lateral Raise

Exercise ID: EX034
Category: Shoulders
Equipment: Dumbbells
Primary muscles: Lateral deltoids

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand tall, soft elbows, dumbbells at sides.
2. Movement: Raise arms out to sides to about shoulder height.
3. Return: Lower slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Swinging; shrugging; thumbs sharply down.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Cable lateral raise. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
