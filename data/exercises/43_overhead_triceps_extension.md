# Overhead Triceps Extension

Exercise ID: EX043
Category: Arms
Equipment: Dumbbell or cable
Primary muscles: Triceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Hold weight overhead, elbows pointing forward.
2. Movement: Bend elbows to lower weight behind head.
3. Return: Extend elbows without arching back.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Flared elbows; painful shoulder range.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Triceps pushdown. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
