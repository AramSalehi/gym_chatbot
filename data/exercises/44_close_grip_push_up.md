# Close-Grip Push-Up

Exercise ID: EX044
Category: Arms
Equipment: Floor or bench
Primary muscles: Triceps, chest

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Place hands moderately narrow, keep body aligned.
2. Movement: Lower chest with elbows near sides.
3. Return: Press back to start.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Hands too close causing wrist pain; sagging hips.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Incline close-grip push-up. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
