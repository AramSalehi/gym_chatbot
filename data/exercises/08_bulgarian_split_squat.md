# Bulgarian Split Squat

Exercise ID: EX008
Category: Legs
Equipment: Bench and optional dumbbells
Primary muscles: Quadriceps, glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Place rear foot on low bench, front foot far enough forward.
2. Movement: Lower hips vertically with weight centered on front leg.
3. Return: Press through front foot to rise.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Overstriding; losing balance; painful rear hip stretch.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Static split squat. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
