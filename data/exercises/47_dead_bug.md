# Dead Bug

Exercise ID: EX047
Category: Core
Equipment: Mat
Primary muscles: Deep abdominals

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Lie on back, hips and knees at 90 degrees, arms up.
2. Movement: Extend opposite arm and leg while keeping trunk stable.
3. Return: Return and alternate sides.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Low back arching; rushing.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Heel taps. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
