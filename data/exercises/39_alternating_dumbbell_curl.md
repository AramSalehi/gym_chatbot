# Alternating Dumbbell Curl

Exercise ID: EX039
Category: Arms
Equipment: Dumbbells
Primary muscles: Biceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand tall holding dumbbells at sides.
2. Movement: Curl one dumbbell, turning palm up comfortably.
3. Return: Lower then switch sides.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Rocking body; rushing.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Cable curl. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
