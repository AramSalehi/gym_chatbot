# Lat Pulldown

Exercise ID: EX023
Category: Back
Equipment: Cable pulldown machine
Primary muscles: Latissimus dorsi, biceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Sit tall, secure thighs, grip bar wider than shoulders.
2. Movement: Pull bar toward upper chest by driving elbows down.
3. Return: Return arms overhead with control.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Pulling behind neck; excessive leaning; jerking.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Assisted pull-up. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
