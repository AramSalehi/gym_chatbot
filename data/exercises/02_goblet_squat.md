# Goblet Squat

Exercise ID: EX002
Category: Legs
Equipment: Dumbbell or kettlebell
Primary muscles: Quadriceps, glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Hold weight close to chest, feet shoulder-width.
2. Movement: Sit between hips, bending knees while keeping torso tall.
3. Return: Push floor away to stand.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Dropping chest; heels lifting.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Bodyweight squat. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
