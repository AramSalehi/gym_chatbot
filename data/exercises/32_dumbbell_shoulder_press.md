# Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Exercise ID: EX032
Category: Shoulders
Equipment: Dumbbells and bench
Primary muscles: Deltoids, triceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Sit or stand tall, dumbbells near shoulder height.
2. Movement: Press upward in comfortable arc.
3. Return: Lower to shoulder level with control.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Overarching low back; shrugging.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Machine shoulder press. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
