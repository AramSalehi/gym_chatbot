# Plank

Exercise ID: EX045
Category: Core
Equipment: Mat
Primary muscles: Abdominals, trunk stabilizers

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Place forearms under shoulders, toes on floor.
2. Movement: Brace abdomen and maintain straight body line.
3. Return: Hold with steady breathing, then rest.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Sagging hips; holding breath.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Knee plank. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
