# Push-Up

Exercise ID: EX019
Category: Chest
Equipment: Floor or elevated bench
Primary muscles: Pectorals, triceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Hands slightly wider than shoulders, body in straight line.
2. Movement: Lower chest toward floor with elbows angled back.
3. Return: Push away until arms straight.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Sagging hips; flared elbows.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Incline push-up. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
