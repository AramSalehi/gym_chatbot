# Seated Leg Extension

Exercise ID: EX009
Category: Legs
Equipment: Leg extension machine
Primary muscles: Quadriceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Adjust pad above ankles and align knee with machine pivot.
2. Movement: Extend knees through comfortable range.
3. Return: Lower slowly without letting stack crash.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Swinging; forcing painful end range.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Sit-to-stand. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
