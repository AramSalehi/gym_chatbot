# Conventional Deadlift

Exercise ID: EX005
Category: Legs
Equipment: Barbell
Primary muscles: Glutes, hamstrings, quadriceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand with midfoot under bar, hinge and grip, brace core.
2. Movement: Push floor away and extend hips and knees together, bar close.
3. Return: Hinge down and bend knees to lower under control.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Jerking bar; rounding back; bar drifting forward.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Kettlebell deadlift. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
