# Barbell Biceps Curl

Exercise ID: EX038
Category: Arms
Equipment: Barbell
Primary muscles: Biceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand tall with elbows near sides.
2. Movement: Bend elbows to lift bar toward chest.
3. Return: Lower slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Swinging hips; elbows drifting forward.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Dumbbell curl. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
