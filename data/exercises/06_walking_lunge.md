# Walking Lunge

Exercise ID: EX006
Category: Legs
Equipment: Bodyweight or dumbbells
Primary muscles: Quadriceps, glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand upright with room to step.
2. Movement: Step forward and lower until both knees comfortably bend.
3. Return: Push through front foot and bring rear leg forward.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Front knee collapsing inward; rushing balance.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Reverse lunge. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
