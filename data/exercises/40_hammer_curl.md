# Hammer Curl

Exercise ID: EX040
Category: Arms
Equipment: Dumbbells
Primary muscles: Brachialis, brachioradialis

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Hold dumbbells with palms facing inward.
2. Movement: Curl while keeping neutral grip.
3. Return: Lower slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Swinging; elbows moving far forward.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Rope hammer curl. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
