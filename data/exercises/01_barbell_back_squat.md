# Barbell Back Squat

Exercise ID: EX001
Category: Legs
Equipment: Barbell and rack
Primary muscles: Quadriceps, glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Set bar across upper back, brace torso, feet about shoulder-width.
2. Movement: Bend hips and knees, descend as far as controlled with heels grounded.
3. Return: Drive through whole foot to stand.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Heels lifting; knees collapsing inward; losing neutral trunk.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Goblet squat. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
