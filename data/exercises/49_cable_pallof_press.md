# Cable Pallof Press

Exercise ID: EX049
Category: Core
Equipment: Cable or resistance band
Primary muscles: Obliques, anti-rotation core

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand side-on to anchor, hold handle at chest.
2. Movement: Press hands forward while resisting rotation.
3. Return: Bring hands back slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Twisting toward anchor; leaning.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Tall-kneeling Pallof press. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
