# Cable Triceps Pushdown

Exercise ID: EX042
Category: Arms
Equipment: Cable machine and rope/bar
Primary muscles: Triceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand tall, elbows tucked beside torso.
2. Movement: Extend elbows to push handle down.
3. Return: Return until forearms rise comfortably.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Swinging shoulders; elbows drifting.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Resistance band pushdown. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
