# Front Raise

Exercise ID: EX035
Category: Shoulders
Equipment: Dumbbells or plate
Primary muscles: Anterior deltoids

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand upright with light weights in front of thighs.
2. Movement: Raise arms forward to about shoulder height.
3. Return: Lower slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Leaning back; swinging.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Landmine press. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
