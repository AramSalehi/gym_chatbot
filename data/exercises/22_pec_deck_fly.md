# Pec Deck Fly

Exercise ID: EX022
Category: Chest
Equipment: Pec deck machine
Primary muscles: Pectorals

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Adjust seat and arms for comfortable shoulder position.
2. Movement: Bring pads/handles together in front of chest.
3. Return: Return slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Excessive stretch; shrugging.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Cable fly. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
