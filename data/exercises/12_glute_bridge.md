# Glute Bridge

Exercise ID: EX012
Category: Legs
Equipment: Mat
Primary muscles: Glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Lie on back with knees bent, feet flat.
2. Movement: Brace abdomen and lift hips by squeezing glutes.
3. Return: Lower pelvis slowly.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Overarching low back; pushing only through toes.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Hip thrust. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
