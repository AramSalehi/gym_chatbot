# Straight-Arm Pulldown

Exercise ID: EX030
Category: Back
Equipment: Cable station
Primary muscles: Lats

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand facing high pulley with arms nearly straight.
2. Movement: Pull handle down toward thighs by moving shoulders.
3. Return: Return slowly overhead.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Bending elbows into triceps pushdown; swinging.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Lat pulldown. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
