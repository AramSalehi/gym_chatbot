# Side Plank

Exercise ID: EX046
Category: Core
Equipment: Mat
Primary muscles: Obliques, lateral trunk

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Lie on side, elbow under shoulder, knees or feet stacked.
2. Movement: Lift hips until body forms straight line.
3. Return: Hold and lower with control.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Shoulder collapsing; hips rotating.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Bent-knee side plank. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
