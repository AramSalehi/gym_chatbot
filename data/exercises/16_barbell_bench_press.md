# Barbell Bench Press

Exercise ID: EX016
Category: Chest
Equipment: Barbell, bench, spotter or safeties
Primary muscles: Pectorals, triceps

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Lie on bench, feet planted, grip just wider than shoulders.
2. Movement: Lower bar under control toward mid-to-lower chest.
3. Return: Press upward while maintaining stable shoulders.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Bouncing bar; elbows flaring excessively; lifting hips.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Dumbbell bench press. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
