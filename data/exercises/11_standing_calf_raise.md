# Standing Calf Raise

Exercise ID: EX011
Category: Legs
Equipment: Calf raise machine or step
Primary muscles: Calves

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand on balls of feet with stable support.
2. Movement: Rise onto toes as high as controlled.
3. Return: Lower heels slowly through comfortable range.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Bouncing; rolling ankles.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Seated calf raise. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
