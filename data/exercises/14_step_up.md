# Step-Up

Exercise ID: EX014
Category: Legs
Equipment: Stable step and optional dumbbells
Primary muscles: Quadriceps, glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Place entire foot on a low stable step.
2. Movement: Push through lead leg to rise onto step.
3. Return: Lower slowly without dropping.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Pushing excessively with trailing foot; knee collapsing.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Low box step-up. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
