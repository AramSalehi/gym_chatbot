# Romanian Deadlift

Exercise ID: EX004
Category: Legs
Equipment: Barbell or dumbbells
Primary muscles: Hamstrings, glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Stand tall with soft knees and weights close to thighs.
2. Movement: Hinge hips backward, keeping weights near legs and spine neutral.
3. Return: Squeeze glutes to return upright.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Rounding back; turning hinge into squat.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Dumbbell hip hinge. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
