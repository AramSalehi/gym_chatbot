# Bird Dog

Exercise ID: EX048
Category: Core
Equipment: Mat
Primary muscles: Spinal stabilizers, glutes

## Step-by-step technique
1. Setup: Start on hands and knees with neutral spine.
2. Movement: Reach opposite arm and leg long without rotating hips.
3. Return: Return and switch sides.
4. Repeat with controlled tempo and comfortable range of motion.

## Common mistakes
Overarching back; twisting hips.

## Beginner modification / alternative
Single-limb reach. Start with light resistance and practice form before adding load.

## Safety guidance
Warm up first. Stop if you experience sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms. Use a qualified trainer for hands-on form feedback. This guide is general education, not medical advice.
