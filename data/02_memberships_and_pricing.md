# Memberships and pricing
All prices are fictional, in USD, taxes included. Memberships are for adults aged 18+.

| Plan | Monthly fee | Access | Classes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $39 | Gym floor during opening hours | Pay per class ($12) |
| Plus | $59 | Gym floor during opening hours | Unlimited standard group classes |
| Premium | $89 | Gym floor during opening hours | Unlimited standard classes and two guest passes/month |

Enrollment fee: $20 once, waived during a trial-to-membership conversion within 7 days. Day pass: $15. Seven-day trial: $25, one per person, no auto-renewal. Student discount: 10% off monthly membership with valid ID; cannot combine with other discounts. Membership dues recur monthly on signup date. Personal training and specialty workshops are not included in any plan.
