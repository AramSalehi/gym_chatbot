IRONHARBOR FITNESS - SAFETY AND ETIQUETTE
Members must be 18 or older. Closed-toe athletic shoes required. Use collars on loaded barbells. Ask staff for a spot when attempting a challenging lift. Stop exercising if you feel unwell and alert staff.
Report damaged equipment immediately. No filming other members without their explicit consent. Clean machines after use and return weights to storage. Food is restricted to the lounge; closed water bottles are allowed on the gym floor.
Emergency procedure: notify staff; staff contact local emergency services as appropriate. The gym has an AED at reception. This document is not medical advice.
